Discover Ruse like a local!

About This Project

Tourism in Ruse is growing, driven by Danube river cruises and tours, but most existing websites and community groups are built for locals, not visitors - and available tourism sites tend to be outdated or unreliable, focusing mainly on descriptions and photos of landmarks rather than practical guidance. Visitors can find out what to see but rarely how to get there, where to find authentic local food, or which hidden gems are worth exploring - and once the standard city-center tour ends, many tourists don't know where to go next, with language barriers making it harder to ask for directions or recommendations.

This project aims to build an interactive map that helps visitors explore the city with confidence - showing landmarks, local restaurants, cafés, bars, and other authentic experiences. Rather than just listing attractions, it focuses on helping tourists understand how to get to a place, what to expect there, and how to discover local culture beyond the standard tourist routes.

How to Get There

Ruse has no international airport of its own, so the closest and most convenient option for most visitors is Bucharest Henri Coandă International Airport (OTP) in Romania, roughly 1.5-2 hours away. From there, you can arrange transport across the border to Ruse - for example:
